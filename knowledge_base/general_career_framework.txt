
UNIVERSAL CAREER DEVELOPMENT FRAMEWORK

1. CAREER EXPLORATION

Career exploration involves identifying professional fields,
occupations, responsibilities, required qualifications,
working environments, and career progression opportunities.

2. EDUCATION AND QUALIFICATIONS

Different professions require different educational backgrounds,
certifications, licenses, vocational training, or practical experience.
Requirements vary by country, employer, and occupation.

3. SKILL ASSESSMENT

Career skill assessment should identify technical skills,
professional skills, communication abilities, practical experience,
transferable skills, and areas requiring improvement.

4. JOB REQUIREMENT ANALYSIS

Job descriptions should be analyzed to identify required skills,
preferred qualifications, education, experience, responsibilities,
tools, and industry-specific knowledge.

5. SKILL GAP ANALYSIS

Compare demonstrated candidate skills against job requirements.
Classify each requirement as:
- Demonstrated
- Partially demonstrated
- Not evidenced
- Unclear

Do not assume a skill exists without supporting evidence.

6. LEARNING ROADMAP

A learning roadmap should include:
- Learning objectives
- Weekly study topics
- Practical exercises
- Projects
- Milestones
- Progress evaluation

7. CAREER TRANSITION

Career transitions should consider transferable skills,
additional education, professional certifications,
experience requirements, and realistic transition steps.

8. INTERVIEW PREPARATION

Interview preparation should cover role-specific knowledge,
behavioral questions, communication, problem-solving,
and practical assessment.

9. PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT

Career development is continuous. Professionals should
regularly review their skills, industry developments,
career goals, and learning progress.

10. RELIABLE GUIDANCE

Distinguish general guidance from verified requirements.
Do not invent salaries, job openings, licenses, certifications,
or country-specific legal requirements.
